BEFORE WE GET STARTED

Common

Can you work with our existing POS software?+

We check your billing software, equipment and network before recommending products. We confirm compatibility before starting the work.

Can you handle multiple outlet openings?+

Yes. We can supply and install CCTV, networks, POS systems and printers across multiple outlets, using an agreed equipment list and setup plan.

Who do we contact if equipment stops working?+

Contact our support team. We help remotely and arrange an engineer visit where needed. Support coverage, contact details and response times are agreed in advance.

Do you provide annual maintenance contracts?+

Yes. We can plan an annual maintenance contract (AMC) based on your equipment, locations and required visits.
